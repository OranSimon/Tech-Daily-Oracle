### KnowAct-GUIClaw: Know Deeply, Act Perfectly, Personal GUI Assistant with Self-Evolving Memory and Skill
- **Authors / Lab:** Yunxin Li, Jinchao Li, Shibo Su, Zhenran Xu, Chenrui Zhao, Tongshu Bian, Xiaoman Liang, Meishan Zhang, Baotian Hu, Min Zhang
